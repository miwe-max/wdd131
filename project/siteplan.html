SkillRise Abia Site Plan

Site Name

SkillRise Abia

Reason: This name directly represents our organization and its mission to bring new (SkillRise Abia) ideas and opportunities to youth and women through innovation and entrepreneurship.

Site Purpose

The SkillRise Abia website serves as a central hub for our mission to empower youth and women through innovation and entrepreneurship. It will:

  • Showcase our programs and initiatives
  • Highlight success stories from our beneficiaries
  • Provide resources for aspiring entrepreneurs
  • Offer a platform for event announcements and registrations
  • Enable newsletter subscriptions for updates
  • Facilitate contact and engagement with our foundation

Scenarios

  1. Scenario 1

    As a young aspiring entrepreneur, how can I find mentorship opportunities through SkillRise Abia?

  2. Scenario 2

    As a potential donor, where can I find information about the impact of SkillRise Abia's programs?

  3. Scenario 3

    As a woman in tech, what resources does SkillRise Abia offer to support my career growth?

Color Schema

  • Primary Dark Green: #087f4f - Used for main background and footer
  • Secondary Dark Green: #05603c - Used for header background and some section backgrounds
  • Secondary Gold/Yellow: #f2a900 - Used for buttons, links, and highlights
  • Dark Green/Black: #17241d - Used for text on dark backgrounds and some section backgrounds
  • White: #ffffff - Used for text on dark backgrounds and some section backgrounds

Typography

  • Headings: Roboto Slab - A serif font for a professional and authoritative look
  • Body: Open Sans - A sans-serif font for easy readability in paragraphs and smaller text
  • Accents: Montserrat - Used for buttons and call-to-action elements
