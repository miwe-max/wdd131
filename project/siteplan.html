SkillRise Abia Site Plan

Site Name

SkillRise Abia

Reason: This name directly represents the organization and its mission to create opportunities for youth and women through skills development, innovation, and entrepreneurship.

Site Purpose

The SkillRise Abia website serves as a central hub for our mission to empower youth and women through innovation and entrepreneurship. It will:

  • Showcase our programs and initiatives
  • Highlight success stories from our beneficiaries
  • Provide resources for aspiring entrepreneurs
  • Offer a platform for event announcements and registrations
  • Enable newsletter subscriptions for updates
  • Facilitate contact and engagement with our foundation

Scenarios

  1. Scenario 1

    As a young aspiring entrepreneur, how can I find mentorship opportunities through SkillRise Abia?

  2. Scenario 2

    As a potential donor, where can I find information about the impact of SkillRise Abia's programs?

  3. Scenario 3

    As a woman in tech, what resources does SkillRise Abia offer to support my career growth?

Color Schema

  • Primary Green: #087f4f - Used for headings, borders, branding elements, and primary accents
  • Primary Dark Green: #05603c - Used for secondary headings and darker brand accents
  • Secondary Gold/Yellow: #f2a900 - Used for highlights, borders, buttons, and call-to-action elements
  • Dark Green/Black: #17241d - Used for primary text, header, and footer backgrounds
  • Light Green/Gray: #f4f8f5 - Used for the main page background
  • White: #ffffff - Used for content backgrounds and text displayed on dark backgrounds
  • Gray: #65736c - Used for secondary text and supporting interface elements
  • Border Light Gray: #dfe7e2 - Used for borders and visual separation between elements

Typography

  • Headings: Roboto Slab - A serif font used to provide a professional and authoritative appearance
  • Body: Open Sans - A sans-serif font used for clear and comfortable reading
  • Accents: Montserrat - Used for buttons, labels, and call-to-action elements
