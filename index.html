Nigeria is a country in West Africa, located along the Gulf of Guinea. It is the most populous country in Africa and is known for its rich cultural diversity. Nigeria has more than 250 ethnic groups, with Hausa, Yoruba, and Igbo being some of the largest. Nigerian culture is colorful and diverse, with many different languages, traditional foods, music, clothing, festivals, and customs. Popular Nigerian foods include jollof rice, pounded yam, egusi soup, and suya. Nigeria is also famous for its music and film industry, especially Afrobeats and Nollywood.
